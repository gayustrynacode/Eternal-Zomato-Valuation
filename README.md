# Eternal Ltd. — Investment Research & Valuation

## Project Overview

An independent undergraduate finance project analysing Eternal Ltd. and its multi-product consumer ecosystem.

The project examines whether the combination of food delivery, quick commerce, going-out services and B2B supplies can create sustainable economic value.

**Research question:** Does Eternal's multi-product ecosystem create economic value, or does it simply bring several businesses under one corporate umbrella?

## Businesses Covered

- **Zomato:** Food delivery
- **Blinkit:** Quick commerce
- **District:** Going-out and entertainment
- **Hyperpure:** B2B restaurant supplies

## Methodology

The project uses publicly available company disclosures and explicitly labelled analyst assumptions to examine:

- Historical financial performance
- Segment-level operating metrics
- Revenue and profitability forecasts
- Discounted cash flow (DCF) valuation
- Sum-of-the-parts (SOTP) valuation
- Scenario and sensitivity analysis
- Customer economics and advertising potential
- Competitive operating metrics versus Swiggy
- Implied valuation of Blinkit

## Key Questions

1. How might Eternal's businesses contribute to future profitability?
2. What growth and margin assumptions are required to support the valuation?
3. How sensitive is estimated value to the assumptions used?
4. What can publicly disclosed customer and operating metrics tell us about the ecosystem?

## Limitations

This is an educational valuation exercise, not investment advice. Forecasts, valuation multiples and scenario assumptions are analytical estimates rather than company guidance unless explicitly identified as such. Public disclosures may not provide enough information to establish actual customer overlap across businesses.

## Author's Note

This project was developed to strengthen my understanding of financial statement analysis, financial modelling, business valuation and investment research. The model is intended to be transparent, understandable and open to further refinement.
